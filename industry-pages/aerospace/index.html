Industry Solutions

Aerospace

Plastic-Craft provides aerospace plastics engineered for safety, efficiency, and long-term performance. Aerospace manufacturers and suppliers trust us because we deliver consistent quality and precision, ensuring every component meets strict industry standards. Our plastics are lightweight, flame- and smoke-resistant, and durable under extreme conditions — making them ideal for both commercial and defense aviation.

By replacing heavier metals with high-performance polymers, aircraft achieve reduced fuel consumption, lower maintenance costs, and improved passenger comfort. From transparent windows and canopies to interior wall panels, luggage bins, wiring conduits, and seals, our materials are designed to withstand the rigors of aerospace applications. With decades of experience and close customer relationships, Plastic-Craft helps operators and OEMs optimize safety, sustainability, and efficiency in flight.

Our Applications

  • Aircraft landing gear components
  • Transparent windows, canopies, and dust covers
  • Fasteners, seals, bushings, and bearings
  • Mirrors and dust covers
  • Electrical and thermal insulators
  • Interior wall panels and luggage compartments
  • Ventilation ducting and collapsible ribs
  • Food trays and tray tables
  • Pipes, tubing, and wiring conduits

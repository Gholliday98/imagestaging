Industry Solutions

Automotive & Transportation

Plastic-Craft supplies plastics that make vehicles lighter, safer, and more fuel efficient. Automotive and transportation companies choose our materials because they provide consistent quality and adaptability across cars, trucks, buses, trains, RVs, and marine applications.

Our plastics reduce vehicle weight while maintaining strength, cutting fuel consumption and emissions. They resist corrosion, impact, and wear, making them ideal for powertrain parts, body panels, glazing, and interior components. Transportation systems benefit from our durable plastics in bus windows, railcar panels, truck liners, and marine decking. Plastic-Craft helps manufacturers achieve long-term reliability, safety, and design flexibility in both consumer and commercial transport.

Our Applications

  • Powertrain and transmission parts
  • Bearings, bushings, and gears
  • Wheel well liners, trunk liners, and bed liners
  • Dashboard and interior panels
  • EV charging stations and housings
  • Truck liners, wear pads, and protective shields
  • Bus and railcar glazing, wall panels, and seating components
  • RV body panels, wear pads, and windshields
  • Marine panels, decking, and seals

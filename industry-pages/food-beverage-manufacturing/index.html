Food & Beverage Manufacturing

Today, plastics are used in a variety of food processing and packaging machinery applications. Their ability to withstand hot and cold temperatures, harsh chemicals, and corrosion makes them perfect for processing vessels, feeders, and conveyor systems. Our plastics also stay inert, not affecting the taste or appearance, making them the best material for many food applications.

Advantages of Using Plastic-Craft

Engineered plastics built for food-safe, high-speed production environments

Lightweight & High-Speed

Lightweight plastics enable high-speed production using smaller drive motors while maintaining a long wear life.

Lubrication-Free

Eliminates lubrication to reduce maintenance requirements and contamination risk in food environments.

Chemical & Heat Resistant

Resistant to hot water, steam, and Clean-In-Place (CIP) and Sanitize-In-Place (SIP) cleaning chemicals.

Transparent Grades

Transparent options available for easy visual monitoring of machinery during operation.

Long Wear Life

Limited wear on food packaging and mating machine parts, extending equipment lifespan.

Regulatory Compliance

Grades that comply with FDA, NSF, 3A Dairy, and other Federal Agency Regulations for food-contact applications.

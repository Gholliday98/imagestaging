Industry Solutions

Mining & Mineral Extraction

Plastic-Craft delivers plastics designed for the harsh environments of mining and mineral processing. Our materials are trusted because they provide extreme abrasion resistance, impact strength, and noise reduction — extending equipment life and improving worker safety.

Mining operators rely on our polymers for conveyor systems, chute liners, truck beds, and chemical delivery systems. Unlike steel, our plastics minimize noise, reduce friction, and are available in static-dissipative or flame-retardant versions for added safety. From coal and ore to precious metal recovery, Plastic-Craft supplies plastics that improve uptime, reduce costs, and handle the toughest applications with consistent performance.

Our Applications

  • Conveyor components: gears, rollers, bushings, and sprockets
  • Wear pads, liners, and chute scrapers
  • Truck beds, hoppers, and car liners
  • Mining pump lantern rings and seals
  • Chemical delivery pipe systems and dewatering systems
  • Leachate systems and vibrating bin dischargers
  • Precious metal recovery and flotation systems
  • Safety glazing, windshields, and protective barriers

Plastic-Craft provides high-strength plastics for protective glazing, ballistic shields, and safety applications where impact resistance and durability are essential. We supply polycarbonate, acrylic, and reinforced plastics engineered for high-security uses, including armored vehicles, storm-rated panels, and bullet-resistant windows.

Our plastics are shatter-resistant, lightweight, and up to 250 times stronger than glass, offering reliable protection in critical environments. With optical clarity, UV resistance, and ease of fabrication, they are used in banks, government facilities, detention centers, and defense applications. Customers count on Plastic-Craft for dependable supply and consistent performance in demanding security installations.

Whether you require riot shields, vehicle glazing, or hurricane-resistant barriers, our plastics provide protection, strength, and long-term durability.

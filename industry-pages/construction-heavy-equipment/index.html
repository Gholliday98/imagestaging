Industry Solutions

Construction & Heavy Equipment

Plastic-Craft delivers plastics that improve performance, safety, and durability in both construction projects and heavy machinery. Builders, contractors, and equipment operators rely on our materials because they are strong, lightweight, and weather resistant — helping to lower costs and extend service life.

For construction, our plastics are used in glazing, skylights, roofing, partitions, and sound barriers, offering impact strength and energy efficiency. In heavy equipment, our polymers withstand abrasive environments, reduce noise, and eliminate the need for lubrication. From skyscrapers and infrastructure to earthmovers and industrial machinery, Plastic-Craft provides plastics that simplify installation, reduce downtime, and ensure long-term reliability.

Our Applications

  • Skylights, glazing, and roofing systems
  • Entry canopies and security windows
  • Sound barriers and interior partitions
  • Wall panels and decorative finishes
  • Piping, wiring jacketing, and caulking
  • Heavy equipment bushings, bearings, and rollers
  • Wear pads, seals, and liners
  • Chutes and guards for industrial equipment
  • Outrigger pads, stairs, and grating

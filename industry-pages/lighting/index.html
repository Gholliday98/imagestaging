Lighting

Plastic-Craft manufactures plastics for advanced lighting systems that require optical clarity, safety, and long-term performance. Our materials are engineered for high light transmission, UV stability, and heat resistance, making them ideal for LED housings, lenses, reflectors, and diffusers.

We supply shatter-resistant plastics that are lighter than glass, flame-retardant, and suitable for both indoor and outdoor environments. From streetlight enclosures and automotive headlights to architectural lighting and retail displays, our plastics reduce maintenance costs while ensuring consistent illumination. Lighting manufacturers, contractors, and designers choose Plastic-Craft because we provide reliable materials that meet performance standards and adapt to the evolving needs of modern lighting technology.

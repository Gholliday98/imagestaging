Industry Solutions

Water & Waste Management

Plastic-Craft manufactures plastics that support safe, sustainable, and efficient water management. Our customers depend on us for durable, corrosion-resistant materials that improve performance in potable water, wastewater treatment, and reclamation systems.

Our plastics are lightweight, leak-free, and easy to install, reducing energy costs and extending system life. They are used in municipal water distribution, irrigation systems, desalination, and sewage treatment facilities. By replacing traditional metal piping, our materials conserve water, reduce pumping costs, and lower long-term maintenance. Plastic-Craft helps municipalities, contractors, and environmental operators achieve reliable infrastructure that protects public health and preserves resources.

Our Applications

  • Potable water pipes, pumps, and valves
  • Irrigation systems and aquaculture equipment
  • Hot and cold water distribution systems
  • Plumbing pipes and fittings
  • Double-contained piping systems
  • Sewage treatment paddles, chains, and guides
  • Desalination plant systems
  • Reclamation and wastewater piping
  • Submerged installations and marine water systems
